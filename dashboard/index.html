Methodology

  1. Comparables. Every protocol in the snapshot that already has a token trading (a market cap and a TVL) is one observation of what the market pays for a dollar of TVL. Exchanges are excluded: their books are not comparable to a protocol’s TVL.
  2. The curve. A least-squares fit of log10(market cap) on log10(TVL) over those comparables. The fit, its R-squared and its sample size are shown above.
  3. Category shifts. Each category’s median residual from the curve, shrunk by sample size, so a category with few comparables stays close to the curve. Categories with fewer than three comparables get no shift.
  4. Blind test. Five-fold cross-validation of the whole model: the comparables are split into five groups by slug, the curve and the category shifts are fitted on four groups, and the fifth group’s market caps are predicted with the growth and maturity multipliers applied, as the board scores a pre-token protocol. Every comparable is predicted once. The median error, the 75th percentile and the share of predictions within 2× are shown above, so you can see how much the model is worth before trusting any single number. “Within 2×” means the prediction lies between half and twice the real market cap (0.5 ≤ predicted/real ≤ 2); a prediction far below the real value does not count.
  5. Growth and maturity. The 7-day TVL change moves the value (clamped to −60% / +120%, half weight). Age moves it from 0.6× for a new protocol to 1× after three years; an unknown age counts as one year.
  6. The board. All 40 watchlist protocols, plus every other rankable pre-token protocol below the snapshot floor, ordered by implied value. Versioned pools and exchanges are never ranked.

What this radar does not do

It never predicts whether or when a protocol issues a token. The snapshot records no token; that is not proof there is none. The implied value is this entry’s own claim about what a token would be worth if the protocol’s record stayed as it is. Farm plans are a starting point, not financial advice: check the protocol’s own pages, never pay to claim, and never sign a blind approval.
